# 摘　要

随着我国城镇化进程的持续推进，住宅小区规模不断扩大，社区物业管理的复杂程度也随之提升。传统物业管理模式主要依赖人工操作和纸质记录，在费用收缴、业主信息管理、维修报修、投诉处理等核心业务环节存在效率低下、信息传递不畅、服务响应迟缓等突出问题，难以满足现代化社区治理的实际需求。

本文设计并实现了一套基于Spring Boot与Vue技术栈的社区物业管理系统。系统采用前后端分离架构，后端基于Spring Boot 2.7.18框架构建RESTful API服务，集成Spring Security与JWT实现身份认证与权限管理，利用MyBatis-Plus简化数据访问层开发；前端基于Vue 3框架，结合Element Plus组件库构建响应式管理界面；数据库采用MySQL 8.0存储全部业务数据，共设计28张数据表。

系统面向系统管理员、物业管理员、业主、维修人员四类角色，实现了用户与权限管理、住户与房产管理、虚拟钱包、费用账单管理、维修工单管理、投诉管理、公告管理、停车管理及数据统计报表等九大功能模块。其中，虚拟钱包支付功能是本系统的创新亮点，通过设计完整的充值、支付密码验证、余额扣除等业务闭环，在避免对接真实第三方支付接口的同时，完整模拟了在线缴费的全流程。

经功能测试验证，系统各模块运行稳定，业务流程完整，能够有效解决传统物业管理中存在的信息孤岛、管理效率低下等问题，对推动社区物业管理数字化转型具有一定的参考价值。

**关键词：** 社区物业管理；Spring Boot；Vue；前后端分离；虚拟钱包

# ABSTRACT

With the continuous advancement of urbanization in China, the scale of residential communities keeps expanding, and the complexity of community property management is also increasing accordingly. The traditional property management mode mainly relies on manual operations and paper records, which has prominent problems such as low efficiency, poor information transmission, and slow service response in core business links such as fee collection, owner information management, maintenance repair, and complaint handling, making it difficult to meet the actual needs of modern community governance.

This paper designs and implements a community property management system based on Spring Boot and Vue technology stack. The system adopts a front-end and back-end separation architecture. The back-end builds RESTful API services based on Spring Boot 2.7.18 framework, integrates Spring Security and JWT for identity authentication and permission management, and utilizes MyBatis-Plus to simplify data access layer development. The front-end is based on Vue 3 framework, combined with Element Plus component library to build a responsive management interface. MySQL 8.0 is used to store all business data, with 28 data tables designed in total.

The system serves four types of roles: system administrator, property manager, owner, and maintenance worker, realizing nine major functional modules including user and permission management, resident and property management, virtual wallet, fee and bill management, maintenance order management, complaint management, announcement management, parking management, and data statistical reports. Among them, the virtual wallet payment function is an innovative highlight of this system. By designing a complete business closed loop of recharging, payment password verification, and balance deduction, it completely simulates the entire online payment process while avoiding the complexity of connecting to real third-party payment interfaces.

After functional testing and verification, each module of the system runs stably with complete business processes, which can effectively solve the problems of information silos and low management efficiency in traditional property management, and has certain reference value for promoting the digital transformation of community property management.

**Key words:** Community property management; Spring Boot; Vue; Front-end and back-end separation; Virtual wallet
